    🏝️ TinySettler

    Speed Catan · 1v1 · first to 8 points

    Looking for an opponent…

    How to play
    • Goal: be the first to reach 8 points.
    • Start: place 2 settlements, each with a road.
    • Resources: each turn the dice roll automatically. Every tile with that number pays its resource to the settlements touching it (cities get 2).
    • Build: on your turn, things you can afford glow on the board. Tap one to build it. The Build list shows what each thing costs and what you’re missing.
    • Points: settlement 1, city 2, Longest Road (5+ in a row) 2, Largest Army (3+ knights) 2, Victory Point cards 1.
    • Trade: swap 4 of one kind for any 1 with the bank, or 3:1 / 2:1 if you have a settlement on a harbour.
    • Robber: on a 7, big hands lose half their cards automatically and the roller moves the robber, which blocks a tile and steals a card.
    • Speed: if you can’t do anything, your turn passes by itself.
