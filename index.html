🏝️ TinySettler

    Speed Catan · 1v1 · first to 8 points

    Looking for an opponent…

    How to play
    • Place 2 settlements and 2 roads to start. Dice roll automatically.
    • On your turn, glowing spots are things you can afford — just click to build.
    • Click a resource in your hand to trade it with the bank (4:1, or better at a harbour).
    • Settlement 1 pt, city 2 pts, longest road (5+) 2 pts, largest army (3+ knights) 2 pts.
    • On a 7, discards happen automatically and the roller moves the robber.
    • If you can’t do anything, your turn passes by itself.
